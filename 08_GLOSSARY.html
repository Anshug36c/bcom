Bilingual Glossary / English–Hinglish Glossary

Core terms across all six Semester I courses

Use the English definition for exam wording and the Hinglish explanation to check understanding. Related ideas are cross-linked by subject page; a glossary definition alone is not a full answer.

Accounting · CMC-101T

Accounting equation
English: Assets = Capital + Liabilities; it expresses the dual effect of transactions and the business’s claim structure.
Hinglish: Business ke resources = owner ka claim + outsiders ka claim. Har transaction ke baad equation balance honi chahiye.
Accrual basis
English: Recognise income when earned and expense when incurred, irrespective of cash timing.
Hinglish: Cash aaya ya nahi, service earn hui ho toh income; kharcha incur hua ho toh expense record karo.
Journal
English: Chronological book of original entry with debit and credit details and narration.
Hinglish: Transactions ki date-wise pehli formal entry wali book.
Ledger
English: Account-wise record where journal entries are classified and balances determined.
Hinglish: Har account—Cash, Sales, Rent—ka apna alag record page.
Trial balance
English: A list of debit and credit ledger balances prepared to test arithmetic agreement.
Hinglish: Ledger balances ki list; totals match hona accuracy ka complete proof nahi.
Depreciation
English: Systematic allocation of an asset’s depreciable amount over its useful life.
Hinglish: Asset ki cost ko uske useful years mein expense ke roop mein baantna; cash fund nahi.
Net realisable value
English: Expected selling price less estimated completion and selling costs.
Hinglish: Bechne par expected net amount, selling/completion costs ghata kar.
Outstanding expense
English: Expense incurred in the period but not yet paid; recognised as expense and liability.
Hinglish: Is saal ka kharcha, payment baaki—expense badhao aur liability dikhao.

Management · CMC-102T

Efficiency
English: Achieving output with economical use of resources and minimal avoidable waste.
Hinglish: Kam waste mein kaam karna; yeh akela sahi goal achieve karne ki guarantee nahi.
Effectiveness
English: Attainment of intended objectives.
Hinglish: Sahi target hasil karna; achha management efficient bhi aur effective bhi hota hai.
Delegation
English: Assignment of duties and grant of authority to a subordinate, while the manager retains ultimate accountability.
Hinglish: Kaam aur uske liye zaroori adhikar dena; final jawabdehi manager se poori tarah transfer nahi hoti.
Span of control
English: Number of subordinates directly reporting to a manager.
Hinglish: Ek manager ke direct reports ki sankhya.
Coordination
English: Harmonising activities and efforts so organisational parts contribute to common objectives.
Hinglish: Alag teams ke kaam, timing aur information ko common goal ke liye align karna.
TQM
English: Organisation-wide continuous-improvement approach focused on customer requirements and quality in processes.
Hinglish: Quality sirf inspection department ki nahi—har process aur har employee ki responsibility.
Management by Objectives
English: A participative approach in which managers and employees agree measurable objectives and review results.
Hinglish: Manager aur employee milkar measurable goals set karte aur progress check karte hain.

Business Economics · CMC-103T

Opportunity cost
English: Value of the next-best alternative forgone when a choice is made.
Hinglish: Jo best doosra option chhoda, uski value hi choice ki opportunity cost hai.
Equilibrium price
English: Price at which quantity demanded equals quantity supplied, other things equal.
Hinglish: Jis price par buyers jitna lena chahte hain, sellers utna hi supply karte hain.
Price elasticity of demand
English: Responsiveness of quantity demanded to a percentage change in price.
Hinglish: Price badalne par demand kitni sensitive hai, iska measure.
Marginal cost
English: Additional total cost of producing one more unit (or a specified extra amount).
Hinglish: Ek extra unit banane se total cost mein jitna izafa ho.
Isoquant
English: Curve showing input combinations that produce the same output level.
Hinglish: Labour-capital ke alag combinations jo same output dete hain.
Inflation
English: Sustained increase in the general price level, reducing purchasing power when income does not keep pace.
Hinglish: Aam prices ka lagatar badhna; ek item ki price rise akeli inflation nahi.
Balance of Payments
English: Systematic record of economic transactions between residents of a country and the rest of the world.
Hinglish: Desh ke residents aur baaki duniya ke beech economic transactions ka record.

Leadership & Teamwork · CMC-104T

Leadership
English: A process of influencing people toward shared objectives, shaped by leader, followers and situation.
Hinglish: Common goal ki taraf logon ko influence karna; sirf designation leadership nahi.
Emotional intelligence
English: Capabilities involving awareness and regulation of emotion, empathy, motivation and relationship management.
Hinglish: Apni aur dusron ki feelings samajh kar response ko constructive rakhna.
Empowerment
English: Providing people with competence, information, resources and discretion to act responsibly.
Hinglish: Kaam karne ke liye skill, information, resources aur suitable decision freedom dena.
Psychological safety
English: Shared belief that people can raise questions, concerns and mistakes without interpersonal humiliation.
Hinglish: Team mein bina beizzati ke doubt, concern ya mistake bol paana—standards phir bhi high rehte hain.
Task conflict
English: Disagreement about ideas, evidence or work content; it can improve decisions if handled respectfully.
Hinglish: Kaam/idea par disagreement; personal attack na bane toh decision ko behtar kar sakta hai.
360-degree feedback
English: Developmental feedback gathered from multiple perspectives such as manager, peers, reports and self.
Hinglish: Alag working relationships se behaviour par feedback, ideally confidential and coach-supported.

Communication Skills · HLC-101T

Feedback
English: Receiver’s response that helps the sender assess whether the message was understood and acted upon.
Hinglish: Receiver ka response jisse pata chale message samjha gaya ya nahi.
Grapevine
English: Informal communication network that spreads information outside formal channels.
Hinglish: Office ki unofficial baat-cheet ka network; fast ho sakta hai par rumour bhi faila sakta hai.
Active listening
English: Attentive listening with paraphrasing, clarifying questions and confirmation of agreed meaning/actions.
Hinglish: Dhyan se sunna, apne words mein confirm karna, sawaal poochna aur next steps clear karna.
Paralanguage
English: Vocal features such as pace, pitch, volume and pauses that accompany spoken words.
Hinglish: Awaaz ka tone, speed, volume aur pause—words ke alawa meaning banate hain.
STAR method
English: Situation, Task, Action, Result structure for concise evidence-based interview examples.
Hinglish: Situation kya thi, task kya tha, aapne action kya liya aur result kya mila—interview answer ka framework.

Environmental Studies · BSE-101T

Ecosystem
English: Interacting community of organisms and their physical environment.
Hinglish: Jeev-jantu, plants, microbes aur unke physical surroundings ka interacting system.
Biodiversity
English: Variety within genes, among species and across ecosystems.
Hinglish: Genes, species aur habitats ki variety.
Endemic species
English: Species naturally restricted to a particular geographic area.
Hinglish: Aisi species jo naturally ek limited region mein hi milti hai.
In-situ conservation
English: Conservation of organisms within their natural habitats and ecological relationships.
Hinglish: Natural habitat mein hi protection, jaise protected area.
Ex-situ conservation
English: Conservation outside the natural habitat, for example in a seed bank or botanical garden.
Hinglish: Natural habitat ke bahar protection, jaise seed bank, zoo ya botanical garden.
Mitigation and adaptation
English: Climate mitigation reduces causes/emissions; adaptation reduces harm from actual or expected impacts.
Hinglish: Mitigation emissions/causes ghataata; adaptation impacts ke saath cope karne ki taiyari karta hai.
Waste hierarchy
English: Preference order that prioritises prevention and reduction before reuse, recycling, recovery and disposal.
Hinglish: Sabse pehle waste banne se roko/ghatao; phir reuse/recycle; disposal last option.
