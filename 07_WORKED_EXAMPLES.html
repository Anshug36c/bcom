A. Accounting — journal, ledger logic and trial balance

Example 1: journal entries and equation

Transactions: (1) owner starts business with cash ₹1,00,000; (2) buys furniture for cash ₹12,000; (3) buys goods on credit from Riya ₹20,000; (4) sells goods for cash ₹8,000; assume goods sold cost ₹5,000; (5) pays rent ₹2,000.

TransactionDebitCredit
Capital introducedCash 1,00,000Capital 1,00,000
Furniture boughtFurniture 12,000Cash 12,000
Credit goods purchasePurchases 20,000Riya 20,000
Cash saleCash 8,000Sales 8,000
Cost of goods sold (per perpetual inventory assumption)Cost of Sales 5,000Inventory 5,000
Rent paidRent 2,000Cash 2,000
Hinglish reasoning: Furniture business-use asset hai, isliye Purchases account nahi. Resale ke liye goods Purchases hain. Sale entry revenue record karti; cost entry tabhi alag hogi jab perpetual inventory method assume ho. Traditional periodic system mein closing stock/COGS final accounts ke waqt nikalte—exam mein apne prescribed method ko follow karein. Har entry mein total debit = total credit.

Example 2: trial balance and what it proves

Suppose ledger balances are Cash Dr ₹94,000; Furniture Dr ₹12,000; Purchases Dr ₹20,000; Cost of Sales Dr ₹5,000; Rent Dr ₹2,000; Capital Cr ₹1,00,000; Riya Cr ₹20,000; Sales Cr ₹8,000; Inventory Cr ₹5,000. Debits total ₹1,33,000; credits total ₹1,33,000. (This abbreviated example includes an inventory credit under perpetual cost recognition.) Agreement confirms arithmetic equality, but if a ₹1,000 credit purchase was completely omitted, both totals might still agree.

Hinglish: Trial Balance match hona ek useful check hai, final proof nahi. Wrong account same side par post ho, transaction chhoot jaaye, ya equal errors offset ho jaayen, TB phir bhi match kar sakta hai.

B. Accounting — final accounts and adjustments

Example 3: Trading Account and gross profit

Opening inventory ₹10,000; purchases ₹60,000; returns outward ₹2,000; direct wages ₹5,000; carriage inward ₹1,000; sales ₹1,00,000; returns inward ₹3,000; closing inventory ₹15,000.

Net purchases = 60,000 − 2,000 = ₹58,000. Net sales = 1,00,000 − 3,000 = ₹97,000. Cost of goods available/sold = opening inventory 10,000 + net purchases 58,000 + direct wages 5,000 + carriage inward 1,000 − closing inventory 15,000 = ₹59,000. Gross profit = net sales 97,000 − COGS 59,000 = ₹38,000.

Hinglish: Pehle returns adjust karke net figures nikalo. Direct costs ko goods ke cost mein include karo. Closing stock minus hota hai kyunki woh abhi sell nahi hua; woh balance sheet asset bhi hai (jab trial balance ke bahar adjustment diya ho).

Example 4: adjustment double effect

Trial balance shows salary ₹24,000 and insurance ₹6,000. At year-end, salary outstanding ₹2,000 and insurance prepaid ₹1,000. Adjusted salary expense = ₹26,000; show ₹2,000 current liability. Adjusted insurance expense = ₹5,000; show ₹1,000 current asset. If depreciation ₹4,000 is also charged on equipment, debit depreciation expense ₹4,000 and credit accumulated depreciation (or reduce equipment carrying amount) ₹4,000.

Hinglish: Outstanding ka matlab service/expense current period ka hai, payment baad mein—expense badhao aur liability banao. Prepaid future benefit hai—expense ghatao aur asset dikhao. Adjustment ka income statement aur balance sheet dono par effect yaad rakho.

C. Accounting — depreciation and inventory

Example 5: SLM and WDV

Machine cost ₹1,20,000; residual value ₹20,000; useful life 5 years. SLM annual depreciation = (1,20,000 − 20,000) ÷ 5 = ₹20,000. At WDV rate 20%, year 1 depreciation = 20% × ₹1,20,000 = ₹24,000; closing carrying amount ₹96,000. Year 2 = 20% × ₹96,000 = ₹19,200; closing amount ₹76,800.

Hinglish: SLM har saal same ₹20,000 charge karta. WDV mein rate same hai, lekin base har saal reduced book value hota hai, isliye charge decline karta. WDV question mein scrap value/rate ka treatment question ke stated method ke anusaar lo.

Example 6: weighted-average inventory

Opening: 10 units @ ₹10 = ₹100. Purchase: 20 units @ ₹13 = ₹260. Total 30 units cost ₹360; weighted-average cost = ₹360 ÷ 30 = ₹12 per unit. If 18 units are issued, closing stock = 12 units × ₹12 = ₹144; issue cost = 18 × ₹12 = ₹216.

Hinglish: Average rate nikalte waqt total cost ko total units se divide karo. Issue aur closing stock dono par wahi rate lagao. FIFO mein instead purchase layers ko order se consume karte hain.

D. Business Economics — demand and elasticity

Example 7: elasticity and total revenue

Price falls from ₹10 to ₹8; quantity demanded rises from 100 to 130. Midpoint percentage change in quantity = 30 ÷ 115 = 26.09%; midpoint percentage change in price = 2 ÷ 9 = 22.22%. Price elasticity magnitude ≈ 26.09 ÷ 22.22 = 1.17, so demand is elastic over this range. Initial revenue = ₹10 × 100 = ₹1,000; new revenue = ₹8 × 130 = ₹1,040, which rises, consistent with elastic demand for this interval.

Hinglish: Midpoint method dono periods ke average ko base banata hai, isliye direction reverse karne par result consistent rehta hai. Elastic demand mein price reduction se total revenue aksar badhta, par real pricing decision mein cost, competition aur customer lifetime effects bhi dekho.

E. Business Economics — cost analysis

Example 8: fixed, variable, average and marginal cost

A firm has fixed cost ₹500. At output 10 units, variable cost is ₹700, so total cost ₹1,200; average total cost = ₹120 per unit; average variable cost = ₹70. If total cost at 11 units is ₹1,270, marginal cost of the 11th unit = ₹70. Always compare marginal cost with average cost: if MC is below ATC, ATC falls; if MC is above ATC, ATC rises.

Hinglish: Fixed cost output ke saath short run mein nahi badalta; variable cost output ke saath badalta hai. Average total cost = total cost ÷ output. Marginal cost = ek extra unit ke karan total cost ka increase. Table/graph mein units aur cost clearly label karo.

F. Management, leadership and communication — applied answers

Case: targets, delegation and control

A retail manager sets a target to reduce stock-outs by 20% in a quarter. She consults staff, identifies the high-demand products, assigns a stock-monitoring owner, gives authority to request replenishment within limits, and schedules weekly review. This combines planning (measurable target), organising (role), delegation (authority with accountability), coordination (sales/procurement), and control (weekly actual-vs-target review). If staff hide stock-outs to protect their score, the measure is poorly designed; pair the metric with customer complaints and inventory accuracy.

Hinglish answer frame: Case ke facts ko function se connect karo. Pehle target/plan identify, phir roles/authority, phir coordination, phir measure aur corrective action. Sirf theory ki definition likhne ke bajay case evidence point karo.

Sample business letter outline

Subject: Request for correction of statement dated 12 March
Dear Sir/Madam,
I am writing regarding account reference [verified reference]. The statement dated [date] appears to include [specific entry] twice. I have attached the relevant statement page and transaction proof. Please review the entry and issue a corrected statement, or let me know if additional information is required. I would appreciate an update by [reasonable date].
Yours faithfully,
[Name and contact]

Hinglish: Letter mein neutral facts, exact reference, supporting proof aur clear requested action hota hai. Real sensitive account details sirf secure channel par bhejein; yeh sample dummy placeholders ke saath hai.

G. Environmental Studies — applied answer

Pollution case using source-to-control chain

Suppose a neighbourhood reports dust and breathing discomfort near a busy construction site. A well-structured response identifies likely sources (uncovered material, vehicle movement, demolition), exposure route (airborne particulate matter), affected groups, evidence needed (monitoring and inspection), and controls (cover stockpiles, wheel washing, water/approved dust suppression, maintain equipment, manage traffic, enforce standards and communicate complaints). Avoid claiming causation from symptoms alone; public-health investigation and reliable monitoring are needed.

Hinglish: Pollution answer ko source → pathway → exposure/impact → prevention/control order mein likho. Evidence aur precaution dono important hain; bina data ke kisi ek source ko conclusively blame mat karo.
