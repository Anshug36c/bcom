Welcome — how to use this kit

This guide and the linked subject notes are based on the six-course B.Com Semester I curriculum PDF found in Drive. The goal is to help you understand the syllabus, revise unit by unit and practise writing answers. Notes are in Hinglish for learning; keep core technical terms in English in your exam answers. Treat these as a study aid, not a replacement for your department’s latest notices, lectures or prescribed readings.

  1. Open a subject page and compare its unit headings with your class syllabus.
  2. Read one concept, close the page, and explain it aloud in your own words.
  3. Work through the example or application; for Accounting, practise the full calculation and format.
  4. Attempt the unit question bank under a time limit, then check missing headings and examples.
  5. Revisit weak points after one day and again after a week.

Official unit map

CMC-101T Introduction to Accounting: (I) concepts, principles, conventions, equations, accounts and recording; (II) journal, ledger and trial balance; (III) trading/P&L, balance sheet and receipts & payments; (IV) depreciation and stock valuation; (V) computers and Tally.
CMC-102T Management: (I) management foundations and Taylor/Fayol; (II) planning and decisions; (III) organising and delegation; (IV) coordination, TQM, MBE, core competence and CSR; (V) direction, control and budgetary control.
CMC-103T Business Economics: (I) foundations, market forces and equilibrium; (II) demand, elasticity and forecasting; (III) production and costs; (IV) market structures and pricing; (V) growth, cycles, BOP and inflation.
CMC-104T Leadership & Teamwork: (I) leadership concepts and development; (II) attributes/personality/EI; (III) power, influence, ethics and behaviour; (IV) delegation, empowerment and team leadership; (V) team processes, coaching, decisions and conflict.
HLC-101T Communication Skills: (I) communication process and barriers; (II) corporate communication, discussion and interviews; (III) business letters; (IV) oral/non-verbal communication, listening, presentations and CV; (V) recruitment/employment correspondence.
BSE-101T Environmental Studies: (I) introductory scope and sustainability; (II) ecology and ecosystems; (III) biodiversity and conservation; (IV) pollution and global environmental issues. The supplied PDF lists four units for this course.

Answer-writing strategy

  • Define first: state the concept accurately in one or two lines.
  • Organise: use headings, numbered stages, comparison tables or labelled diagrams.
  • Apply: give a relevant business, accounting or local/environmental example.
  • Show work: Accounting and Economics numericals need formulas, substitutions, units and workings.
  • Conclude: answer the question directly; avoid unrelated filler.
  • Check: units, dates, debit-credit totals, diagram labels and all subparts.
